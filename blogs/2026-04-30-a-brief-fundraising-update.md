We’re excited to share that we’ve raised $75M in new funding from Sequoia and Spark Capital—partnering with Sonya Huang, Mikowai Ashwill, and Yasmin Razavi, all of whom are incredibly thoughtful and deeply supportive of our long-term mission of building aligned AGI. We’ve also brought on angels & advisors including Milan Kovac, Stanley Druckenmiller, and Andrej Karpathy. Our early results with FDM-1 moved computer use from a data-constrained regime to a compute-constrained one; this latest round of funding unlocks several orders of magnitude of compute scaling for that work.
